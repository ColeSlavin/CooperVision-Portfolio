# Supply Chain Analytics SQL Portfolio

## Overview
This project demonstrates how SQL can be used to drive actionable insights in supply chain management. It defines key performance indicators (KPIs), risk metrics, and analytical queries that form the foundation for dashboards and reports in corporate settings. The notebook is structured into thematic sections for clarity and business relevance.

## Contents
- `schema.sql` → Table creation script for the raw dataset.
- `queries.sql` → Analytical queries organized by theme.
- `README.md` → Documentation and business context.

## Key Sections

### Supplier Analytics
- Reliability by country  
- Supplier performance scorecards  
- High-risk supplier monitoring  
- Supplier ranking within country  

### Risk Validation
- Validate risk classifications  
- Risk distribution by country  
- Identify disruption-prone suppliers  

### Inventory & Demand
- Inventory-to-demand ratio (stockout risk)  
- Fill rate by product  

### Logistics & Cost
- Countries with slowest lead times  
- Most expensive shipping lanes  
- Cost vs reliability correlation  

### Volatility & External Drivers
- Delivery time volatility  
- Weather severity impact  
- Route risk vs performance  

### Product Stability & KPIs
- Product-level supply chain stability  
- Service level (on-time percent)  

## Business Impact
These queries demonstrate how SQL can:
- Identify risky suppliers and validate risk models  
- Prevent stockouts with inventory-to-demand ratios and fill rate analysis  
- Improve service levels by monitoring delay probability and lead times  
- Optimize logistics lanes by analyzing cost, volatility, and external drivers
